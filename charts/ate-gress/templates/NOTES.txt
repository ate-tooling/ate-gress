ate-gress is serving ext_proc on {{ include "ate-gress.name" . }}.{{ .Release.Namespace }}.svc:{{ .Values.grpcPort }}.

Point Substrate's egress gateway at it. With the agent-substrate-operator:

  kubectl patch substrateinstall substrate --type merge \
    -p '{"spec":{"atenet":{"additionalEgressExtprocService":"{{ .Release.Namespace }}/{{ include "ate-gress.name" . }}:{{ .Values.grpcPort }}"}}}'

With ate-setup:

  ate-setup deploy ate-system --experimental-additional-egress-extproc-service={{ .Release.Namespace }}/{{ include "ate-gress.name" . }}:{{ .Values.grpcPort }}

The gateway fails closed: once it is pointed here, its HTTP egress depends on
ate-gress being up. Policies are EgressRoutePolicy objects:

  kubectl get egressroutepolicies
